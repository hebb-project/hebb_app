# Aphrodite

Aphrodite, goddess of love and beauty, born from the sea-foam at
Cyprus after the castration of Uranus (or, in [[homer]], daughter
of [[zeus]] and Dione). Mother of [[aeneas]] by Anchises. Roman
[[venus]].
