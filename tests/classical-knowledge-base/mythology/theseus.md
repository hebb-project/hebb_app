# Theseus

Theseus, mythical hero-king of [[athens]], slayer of the Minotaur
in the Cretan labyrinth, unifier of Attica. The Athenians treated
him as their founder-hero — their answer to the
[[romulus-and-remus|Roman]] Romulus.
