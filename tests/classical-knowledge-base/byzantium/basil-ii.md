# Basil Ii

Basil II (958–1025), called *Boulgaroktonos* — "Bulgar-Slayer" —
was a Byzantine emperor who crushed the First Bulgarian Empire,
stabilized the eastern frontier, and brought the
[[byzantine-empire]] to its medieval peak.
